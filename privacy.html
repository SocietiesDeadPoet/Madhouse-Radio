Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026

Madhouse Radio is a Twitch channel panel operated by DamadHattah. The extension itself does not request Twitch viewer identity, collect or store personal information, use cookies, maintain user accounts, or operate its own analytics or tracking system.

Madhouse Radio provides a link to a public Suno playlist. When you choose to open that link, you leave the extension and interact with Suno, a separate third-party service. Your use of Suno is governed by Suno's own terms and privacy practices. Twitch may separately process information as part of operating its platform.

If Madhouse Radio's data practices materially change, this policy will be updated before those practices are introduced.

Privacy questions may be sent to the support email listed on the Madhouse Radio Twitch Extension listing.
